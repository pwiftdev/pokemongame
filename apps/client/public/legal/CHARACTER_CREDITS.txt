GAME ADAPTATIONS
Heads extracted from the complete bases; modular clothing, hair and head skins
mapped to a shared rig. Animations mapped to the game's existing motion system.
Textures resized to at most 512px and converted to WebP; unused data pruned.
Human, Elf, Dwarf and Orc variations use derived proportions, head geometry,
ear shapes, skin palettes and added tusks. Face/jaw/nose adjustments, beard
length, eyebrow thickness and material colors are game-side adaptations.
These race variants are not separate authored Quaternius race models.

Assembly: scripts/acquire-characters.py and scripts/character-gltf.py.
Source checksums: scripts/data/character-sources.json.
Shipped asset checksums and byte sizes: ASSET_MANIFEST.json.
No paid model pack, ongoing asset subscription or external runtime asset host.
